The Hahnemann Clinic

Becoming a new patient

Treatment begins with a considered process, not a one-tap booking. Here is what to expect — and why the steps are deliberate.

Why there's an application. The intake forms and fee are an intentional filter. They ensure we work with patients who are ready to commit to the timeline of sequential treatment — which is what makes the results lasting.

Read the New Patient Information Package

The NPIP explains how treatment works. It's downloadable or available by mail.

Contact the clinic office

Reach out with questions or to begin. Reception is available Tuesday–Thursday, 9am–4pm EST.

Initial consultations

Submit your intake forms online, then book your appointment. After your first appointment you'll receive The Journey to Health guide.

Group rates

Special rates are available for couples and families being treated together.

Follow-ups

Scheduled regularly to work through your timeline and assess progress.

Between consultations

An On-Call Practitioner is available Monday–Friday during business hours for dosage and remedy questions (small fee).

Payment

Credit card, PayPal, or cheque, depending on your location.

Request the New Patient Package

Leave your details and we'll send the NPIP and next steps. (Prototype form — not yet wired to a backend.)

Case Study

Sam's case

One family's documented experience of two-year sequential Heilkunst treatment — from severe asthma, chronic reflux, and recurrent infections to symptom-free.

By Patty Smith-Verspoor · Individual results vary

"Sam" (born February 1993) was referred by a chiropractor. His chief complaints were recurrent viral infections, chronic gastroesophageal reflux, and severe asthma with stridor.

The timeline of causes

Before birth

Mother had mononucleosis and was prescribed Augmentin; military-related stress; ICU nursing.

Labour & delivery

Difficult labour, transverse head, epidural, C-section, hematoma, circumcision, and a first Hep B vaccination.

Infancy

HIB vaccination at 7 weeks; DPTP at 2 months; MMR at 18 months — followed by the onset of asthma symptoms, then years of hospital visits and medications (Pediapred, Ventolin, Benadryl, Zantac, and more).

The sequential approach

Each layer was treated in reverse order, with remedies matched to specific traumas:

  • Most recent trauma: Arnica, Staphysagria, Opium, Nux vomica
  • Medications around 23 months: Cortisone, Penicillinum, Nux vomica
  • Vaccine layers: MMR, DPTP, Meningiotoxinum (Hib), Hepatitis B — with supporting emotional remedies
  • Miasmic remedies: Psorinum, Tuberculinum, Medorrhinum, Carcinosin, Syphilinum
  • Birth and pre-birth trauma remedies, with emotional support throughout

After about two years

  • No medications
  • No food or environmental allergies
  • Eating meat, eggs, and new foods freely
  • Normal weight, energy, and sleep
  • Social, friendly, charming
  • No asthma, no reflux, no stridor

"To look at him you would never know his history." — Sam's mother

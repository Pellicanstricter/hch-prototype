Patient guide

Building your traumatic timeline

As part of treatment, we ask you to build a timeline of shocks and traumas — either forward from conception and birth, or backward from the present, whichever is easier for you.

An example

2002
Fall requiring stitches, freezing
2000
Emotional — move to Ottawa — sadness, anxiety
1997
Travel vaccinations — Hep B, Yellow Fever (vaccine reaction, illness)

What to include

Physical traumas

  • Pre-birth drugs, alcohol, smoking, severe illness in the mother; ultrasound
  • Birth: difficult labour, forceps, anaesthetics, oxygen deprivation
  • Vaccinations (dates of each type)
  • Accidents: car accidents, falls, concussions, broken bones, animal bites
  • Surgeries: tonsils, appendix, dental, C-sections, vasectomy
  • Drugs: antibiotics, anti-depressants, recreational drugs
  • Hormones: birth control, HRT, IVF
  • Severe infections: Lyme, mono, EBV, measles, chicken pox, TB, pneumonia
  • Electrical shocks

Mental & emotional traumas

  • Loss, abandonment, grief, betrayal (death, job loss, breakups)
  • Great fear, anxiety, stress
  • Anger, indignation, humiliation, guilt
  • Envy, jealousy, self-blame, shame
  • Abuse — mental, emotional, or sexual

Don't worry about getting it perfect. The practitioner will work through it with you — the timeline is a living document you'll refine together as treatment progresses.

Ready to begin treatment?

The timeline is built during your intake. Start with the new-patient process.

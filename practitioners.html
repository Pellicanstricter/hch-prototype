Our practitioners

All our practitioners are graduates and tutors of the Hahnemann College for Heilkunst, registered as Doctors of Homeopathic Medicine and Doctors of Medical Heilkunst.

CoCo Verspoor

DHHP, DMH · Doctor of Medical Heilkunst

CoCo offers a unique holistic healthcare service, combining Heilkunst homeopathy, sequential timeline treatment, holistic nutrition, and movement therapy — designed to improve overall physical and emotional health. She currently focuses her practice on allergies and traumas for women and children.

She is a graduate of the Hahnemann College for Heilkunst and the Spring Valley Eurythmy School, a faculty member at the College, and Program Director at the School for Romantic Science and Healthcare.

Rudi Verspoor

DMH · CoRe Bioresonance Practitioner · SCENAR Therapist

Rudi has two decades of extensive clinical experience treating complex and chronic illnesses, and has written several books based on his research and clinical experience. He previously directed the British Institute of Homeopathy Canada (1993–2001), helped found NUPATH and the International Heilkunst Association, and advises the Canadian government on health-care policy.

Selected publications: AUTISM, The Journey Back; Homeopathy Renewed (with Patty Smith); A Time for Healing; Homeopathy Re-examined and The Dynamic Legacy (both with Steven R. Decker).

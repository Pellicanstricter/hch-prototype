Heilkunst & autism

"There is no question that we currently face an unprecedented tragedy in the form of a profound disturbance in the health of our children — at all levels: body, mind, soul and spirit."

Autism is a disease condition, not a fixed diagnosis

Labels like Autism, ADD, ADHD, PDD and ODD describe behavior — not root causes. Our position is that these conditions arise from an accumulation of inherited weaknesses, traumas, and shocks. When those are removed, in sequence, the system can heal.

The causes Heilkunst addresses

  • Inherited weaknesses from the parents' health
  • Stresses and traumas during pregnancy (ultrasounds, drugs, emotional stress)
  • Birth interventions — induction, epidural, C-section, forceps
  • Vaccinations, particularly early ones (e.g. Hep B)
  • Accidents — concussions, spinal injuries
  • Emotional shocks — deaths, fears, suppressed emotions
  • Iatrogenic disease — drugs, antibiotics, surgeries
  • Poor diet and chemical exposure

On heavy metals: Our research suggests that traumas and inherited weaknesses create a form of "armoring" at the cellular level. Once those traumas are removed, the cells allow toxins to leave and be processed by the body's normal detoxification system.

What treatment looks like

Sequential treatment uses non-toxic, non-invasive, curative medicines — each remedy matched to a particular trauma, applied according to natural-law principles.

  • Approximately 1–2 years to complete the first level
  • In our experience, many families report improvement in the first 3–6 months
  • Because the approach works on underlying causes rather than symptoms, improvements tend to hold

Every case is different, and individual results vary. This reflects our clinical experience, not a guaranteed outcome.

"We were amazed at how much our son changed over two years of treatment." — parent of a child treated for autism

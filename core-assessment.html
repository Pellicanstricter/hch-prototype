Assessment tool

CoRe bio-resonance assessment

A non-invasive assessment tool we use alongside treatment — sometimes called bio-resonance or radionics — to help map where to focus, especially when progress has stalled.

What it is

The CoRe is a non-invasive assessment tool the Clinic uses to look at health and disturbance at the energetic level, rather than only the biochemical one. The underlying idea is that living systems radiate a field of energy, and that disturbances in that field can be observed and worked with.

How it's used

An assessment can be done in person or at a distance. The results are sent to your practitioner, who interprets them and uses them to shape regimenal recommendations and additional remedies within your wider Health Plan.

  • Recommended at the start of treatment, to help establish a baseline
  • Repeated every 3–6 months to track change
  • Especially useful when treatment reaches a standstill, to help find what to address next

A complement, not a diagnosis. The CoRe supports the practitioner's judgment and the wider sequential treatment — it doesn't replace clinical assessment, and it isn't used to diagnose disease.

Bio-resonance is part of the Heilkunst tradition's approach and is not a conventionally recognized diagnostic method. It is used here as one input among many, alongside a qualified practitioner's care, and is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.

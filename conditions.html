The Hahnemann Clinic

Treatment for chronic and complex conditions

We are the international center for the practice of medical Heilkunst — a comprehensive, integrated treatment using safe, non-toxic remedies prescribed according to natural-law principles. Patients come from all over the world; treatment is available in person and by distance.

Conditions we work with

We are a general practice and do not specialize in any one area, though we have a particular interest in childhood disorders — especially autism and other learning and behavioral problems — as well as the treatment of animals.

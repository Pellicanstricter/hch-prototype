The Hahnemann Clinic

Become a member of the Clinic

Heilkunst treatment is a relationship, not a single appointment. Patients join the Clinic as members — which is how we keep care continuous, careful, and genuinely effective.

Sequential Heilkunst treatment unfolds over months and often years, working back through a lifetime of shocks and traumas in order. That kind of care only works as an ongoing partnership. Membership formalizes that partnership, so that you and your practitioner are both committed to the journey ahead.

Why membership

Continuity of care

Treatment is sequential. Your practitioner holds your full timeline and works through it with you, visit after visit — not a one-off consultation.

A considered commitment

The membership step ensures both you and your practitioner are invested before treatment begins — a natural continuation of the intake process.

Ongoing support

Members reach the On-Call Practitioner between consultations for dosage and remedy questions, and for healing-reaction support.

A community of healing

Membership connects you to the wider patient community and the resources of the Center, supporting your health over the long term.

Your Health Plan — putting it all together

Membership gives you a personal Health Plan: a set of elements that work together to address the underlying causes of ill health, turned into a systematic, rational, and integrated plan specific to you.

1

Dietary protocols

A diet matched to your unique biochemistry, life-work style, and typology (blood, glandular, metabolic) — specific advice on the foods that are optimal for you.

2

Trauma removal

A specific sequential treatment, built from your timeline, to remove the blockages left by the shocks and traumas in your life.

3

Inherited issues

Inherited chronic disease conditions (miasms) identified within a scientific framework and treated systematically.

4

Metabolic balancing

Imbalances in your physiological control functions — identified with simple at-home procedures — corrected with a clear protocol.

5

Mineral balancing

Guided by an independent lab analysis, we establish your key mineral balance, which then drives the correction of other nutrient imbalances.

What membership includes

  • Initial intake and main consultation with a practitioner
  • Your personal traumatic timeline, built and maintained with you
  • Regularly scheduled follow-up consultations
  • On-Call Practitioner access, Monday–Friday business hours
  • The Journey to Health guide after your first appointment
  • Group rates for couples and families treated together
  • Access to member resources and the patient community
  • Treatment in person (Ottawa & Montreal) or by phone, Skype & email worldwide

How to join

Read the New Patient Information Package

The NPIP explains how Heilkunst treatment and membership work. Downloadable or available by mail.

Apply & complete intake

Submit your intake forms online. Taking this step thoughtfully helps us give you the best possible start.

Become a member & book

Once your application is reviewed, you join as a member and book your initial consultation.

Begin your timeline

Your first consultations build your timeline and begin sequential treatment, with follow-ups scheduled as you go.

Not sure yet? You don't have to commit to understand. Start free with the first-aid course, or read how Heilkunst works first. Membership is there when you're ready.

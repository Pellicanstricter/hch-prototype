Heilkunst for animals

The same natural-law principles that guide our work with people apply to animals. Heilkunst treatment can support pets and working animals through trauma, chronic illness, and behavioral change.

Animals carry their own histories — accidents, surgeries, vaccinations, losses, and the inherited predispositions beneath them. Like people, they can be treated sequentially, working back through the shocks on their timeline with safe, non-toxic remedies.

What we treat in animals

We see a range of acute and chronic conditions, as well as behavioral and emotional difficulties that often trace back to specific traumas. We have a particular interest in horses and companion animals, and in the emotional life of animals — which is richer, and more relevant to their health, than is often assumed.

"Animals feel grief, fear, and attachment as we do. Their health reflects their histories just as ours does."

Building a timeline for an animal

As with people, treatment begins with a timeline — a chronological record of the shocks and traumas in the animal's life. An owner who knows their animal's history (a rescue's background, a working horse's injuries, a sudden change in temperament) gives the practitioner what they need to begin.

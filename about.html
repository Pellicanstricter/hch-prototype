About the Hahnemann Center

A federally-registered charitable trust dedicated to the preservation, generation, and dissemination of knowledge about Heilkunst — the complete system of medicine founded by Dr. Samuel Hahnemann.

Three integrated bodies

The Center

The charitable trust that preserves and advances the knowledge and practice of Heilkunst.

The College

Education and training for students, practitioners, and the general public — founded 1997.

The Clinic

The therapeutic arm — vital both for ongoing research and for training practitioners.

What is Heilkunst?

Heil = whole, sound, remedial. Kunst = art. Heilkunst is the name Dr. Hahnemann gave to the complete system of medicine and health found in his writings — most notably the Organon der Heilkunst. Homeopathy is only one part of this remarkable system.

It encompasses nutrition, exercise, and lifestyle; therapies that support natural healing; medicine for first-aid, acute and chronic disease; and spiritual evolution. To be a true physician is to be a Heilkünstler — one who practices the art of making people whole at all levels.

"Each and every one of us already contains all the wisdom of the universe within — but we need guidance to bring this inner knowing to conscious knowing." — Rudi Verspoor, Dean

Founded on principle

For nearly three decades the HCH has trained students drawing on over 200 years of principle and clinical experience. Heilkunst is grounded in natural law — principled, patient-oriented, and holistic in outlook. Knowledge over information; understanding over memorization.
